# Product experiences: Angela (source: beauty notes)

[provenance: told_me]

- CeraVe Moisturizing Cream: 9/10. Very hydrating, no reaction. Would repurchase: yes. My staple.
- La Roche-Posay Toleriane Double Repair Face Moisturizer: 8/10. Gentle, fragrance-free. Would repurchase: yes.
- SkinCeuticals C E Ferulic (L-ascorbic acid vitamin C serum): 3/10. Stung and left me red for a day. Would repurchase: no.
- A second L-ascorbic acid vitamin C serum I tried in spring: 3/10. Same stinging. I think high-strength L-ascorbic acid does not suit me.
- Estee Lauder Double Wear Stay-in-Place Foundation (matte): 2/10. Flat and cakey, clung to dry patches. Returned it. Would repurchase: no.
- Ilia Super Serum Skin Tint SPF 40: 8/10. Lovely natural finish, but coverage is too light for dinner or photos.
- Kosas Revealer Concealer: 9/10. Creamy, never creases on me. Would repurchase: yes.
- The Ordinary Hyaluronic Acid 2% + B5: 7/10. Fine, cheap, does the job.
