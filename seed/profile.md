# Beauty profile: Angela (source: beauty notes, told to GlamBought directly)

[provenance: told_me]

## About
- Angela, 32, Asian American woman living in Taiwan. Taiwan is hot and humid.
- Skin type: dry to combination. Overall her skin is very smooth, radiant and healthy; the aim is to keep it that way.
- At home in the humid climate she wants lighter, purely hydrating textures rather than heavy emollients.

## Skin concerns, in her words
- Occasional hormonal pimples around her period.
- Hydration.
- Sun protection: she really wants to minimise UV ageing of the skin.
- Wrinkle prevention, especially around the eyes and the smile lines. Firmness.
- Hyperpigmentation from old pimple scars.
- Excess sebum on the nose.
- Lip care.

## Monthly goals
- Anti-ageing, hydration, sun protection, and alleviating pimples and hyperpigmentation each month.

## Hero ingredient
- She REALLY likes a high concentration of PDRN (polydeoxyribonucleotide) as part of both her day and her night skincare. Prefer products where PDRN is a headline ingredient.

## Values and budget
- Price sensitivity: medium. She wants the best bang for buck in terms of ingredient cleanliness and ingredient strength.
- Less focus on prestige beauty: it tends to have too much fragrance, and money wasted on packaging and marketing. She prefers to avoid heavily fragranced products.
- More focus on functional beauty, especially K-beauty with elite ingredients and real effectiveness at a low price.

## Travel, about once a month
- Climate-specific requests come up depending on the destination, so check her calendar for upcoming trips.
- Drier destinations (for example San Francisco): she needs more emollient moisturisers to prevent dryness.
- High elevation, beach or strong sun (for example Hawaii or Switzerland): reef-friendly and lake-friendly, non-toxic sunscreen, high UV protection, and sunscreen sticks or sprays for easy reapplication. Hiking at elevation means drier air and stronger UV, so stronger face sunscreen plus a reef-friendly body sunscreen for swimming.

## Who she shops for
- Most often herself, then her female friends, her family, and babies.

## What the agent should anticipate, before she asks
- Upcoming trips on her calendar, and what the destination's climate asks of her skin.
- Friends' birthdays and events such as bachelorettes and baby showers.
- Her health data from the Oura ring and the iPhone Health app.
