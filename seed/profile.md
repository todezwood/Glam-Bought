# Beauty profile: Angela (source: beauty notes, told to GlamBought directly)

[provenance: told_me]

- Skin: dry and sensitive. Flushes easily. Occasionally acne-prone along the jaw.
- Home: San Francisco. Cool, foggy, low humidity indoors.
- Colour analysis: Dark Winter. Cool-neutral undertone. Medium-deep shade range.
- Hard rules: fragrance-free only (parfum/fragrance gives me redness). I avoid denatured alcohol (alcohol denat.) because it dries me out.
- Foundation: I want medium coverage with a natural or satin finish. Matte foundations look flat and cakey on my dry skin. I do not buy matte.
- Budget style: usually mid-range. I happily splurge on serums and on a foundation that is genuinely better. I save on cleanser, eye cream and mascara.
- Preferred retailers: Sephora first (I collect Beauty Insider points), then Ulta, then Dermstore.
- Brands I trust: CeraVe, La Roche-Posay, Ilia, Kosas.
